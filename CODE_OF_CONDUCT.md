# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous
place to collaborate.

Participants must avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
unwanted sexual attention, and knowingly disruptive behavior. They should give
constructive feedback, assume good faith, and prioritize the safety and
privacy of users whose data may pass through an example.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through
GitHub. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
